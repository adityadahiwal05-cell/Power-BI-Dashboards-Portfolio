# ⚡ Extreme Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
Created an interactive Sales Analytics Dashboard in Power BI to analyze sales performance, profit trends, customer behavior, and business insights through dynamic visualizations.

The dashboard helps businesses monitor key metrics, identify growth opportunities, and make data-driven decisions for improved performance and profitability.

---

## 🚀 Dashboard Highlights
- Sales Amount, Profit & Quantity KPIs
- Region-wise Sales Performance
- Monthly Sales & Profit Trends
- Customer Purchase Insights
- Payment Mode Analysis
- Category & Sub-category Comparison
- Business Growth Tracking

---

- Power BI

- Data Modeling
- Business Analytics
- Interactive Dashboard Design

## 🎯 Purpose
The objective of this project is to convert raw business data into meaningful insights using interactive dashboards and modern visualization techniques.

This dashboard helps track business performance, analyze customer behavior, monitor profit trends, and improve strategic decision-making.

---

## 📈 Features
- Interactive Filters & Slicers
- Dynamic KPI Cards
- Sales & Profit Trend Analysis
- Customer & Payment Insights
- Region-wise Performance Monitoring
- Visual Data Storytelling
