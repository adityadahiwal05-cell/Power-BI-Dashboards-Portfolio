# 📊 Master Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
Created a powerful and interactive Sales Analytics Dashboard in Power BI to analyze sales performance, profit trends, customer behavior, and overall business growth through dynamic visualizations.

The dashboard helps businesses monitor key performance metrics, identify sales patterns, and make data-driven decisions for improved profitability and operational efficiency.

---

## 🚀 Dashboard Highlights
- Sales Amount, Profit & Quantity KPIs
- Monthly Sales & Profit Analysis
- Region-wise Sales Performance
- Customer Purchase Insights
- Category & Sub-category Analysis
- Payment Mode Distribution
- Business Growth Trend Monitoring

## 🎯 Purpose
The objective of this project is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights using interactive dashboards and modern analytical reporting techniques.

This dashboard helps track sales growth, analyze profit trends, understand customer purchasing behavior, and support strategic business decision-making.
