# 📊 LITA Capstone Customer & Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
Created an interactive Customer & Sales Analytics Dashboard in Power BI to analyze customer behavior, sales performance, revenue trends, and business insights through dynamic visualizations.

The dashboard helps businesses monitor key sales KPIs, understand customer purchasing patterns, and make data-driven decisions for improved growth and performance.

---

## 📑 Dashboard Pages Explanation

### 📊 Slide 1 – Sales Analytics Dashboard
The first dashboard slide focuses on overall sales performance and business insights. It provides a clear overview of sales trends, revenue generation, customer purchases, and regional performance through interactive visualizations.

#### 🔹 Key Insights Covered
- Total Sales & Revenue KPIs
- Monthly Sales Trend Analysis
- Product-wise Sales Performance
- Region-wise Business Insights
- Customer Purchase Behavior
- Sales Growth Monitoring

This slide helps businesses track sales performance, identify profitable areas, and make data-driven decisions for business growth.

---

### 👥 Slide 2 – Customer Analytics Dashboard
The second dashboard slide focuses on customer behavior, segmentation, and engagement insights. It helps analyze customer trends and understand purchasing patterns using interactive charts and KPI visuals.

#### 🔹 Key Insights Covered
- Customer Segmentation Analysis
- Customer Purchase Trends
- Repeat vs New Customers Insights
- Customer Distribution Visualization
- Product Preference Analysis
- Customer Engagement Monitoring

This slide helps businesses understand customer behavior, improve customer experience, and optimize marketing and sales strategies using data-driven insights.
