# 🚀 Advanced Dashboards

## 📌 Overview
Created a collection of advanced and interactive Power BI dashboards designed to analyze business performance, sales forecasting, customer insights, financial transactions, and product analytics through modern data visualization techniques.

These dashboards transform raw datasets into meaningful business insights using dynamic reports, KPI tracking, forecasting models, and intelligent analytics solutions.

---

## 📊 Dashboards Included

### 🛒 Supreme Store Sales Dashboard
An interactive sales analytics dashboard designed to monitor sales performance, profit trends, customer behavior, and business growth insights.

### 📈 Supreme Store Sales Forecasting Dashboard
A forecasting dashboard created to predict future sales trends, analyze business growth patterns, and support strategic planning using predictive analytics.

### 💳 Credit Card Customer Dashboard
A customer analytics dashboard focused on customer demographics, spending behavior, and credit card usage insights.

### 💰 Credit Card Transaction Dashboard
A financial analytics dashboard developed to monitor transaction volumes, payment trends, revenue insights, and banking performance metrics.

### 👥 LITA Capstone Customers Dashboard
A customer insights dashboard designed to analyze customer engagement, purchase behavior, and business performance through dynamic visualizations.

### 🚗 Mahindra EV Sales Dashboards
A collection of electric vehicle analytics dashboards created to track EV sales performance, customer demand, and product growth insights for Mahindra electric vehicles.

### 💍 Shivshakti Jewellers Sales Dashboard
A jewellery business analytics dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, product trends, customer purchases, and revenue insights.

### 📊 Product & Payment Analysis Dashboard
An interactive dashboard focused on product performance, payment methods, sales distribution, and customer transaction analysis.

### 🥇 Gold vs Silver Sales Insights Dashboard
A comparative analytics dashboard created to analyze gold and silver product sales trends, customer preferences, and profitability insights.

### 📍 Pune Area-wise Sales Performance Dashboard
A regional sales analytics dashboard designed to compare sales performance across different areas in Pune through dynamic visual reports.

### 📉 Business Performance Insights Dashboard
A business intelligence dashboard focused on KPI monitoring, operational performance tracking, and strategic business insights.
