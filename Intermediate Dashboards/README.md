# 📊 Intermediate Dashboards

## 📌 Overview
Created a collection of intermediate-level Power BI dashboards focused on sales analytics, HR insights, entertainment analytics, customer feedback analysis, healthcare reporting, and business intelligence solutions through interactive visualizations.

These dashboards demonstrate advanced KPI tracking, trend analysis, forecasting, customer insights, and data storytelling techniques using Power BI and DAX.

---

### 🛒 Supreme Sales Dashboard
An interactive sales analytics dashboard designed to monitor sales amount, profit, quantity, customer insights, and payment mode analysis through dynamic visualizations.

### 👨‍💼 HR Analytics Dashboard
A workforce analytics dashboard focused on employee attrition, salary trends, department insights, and employee demographics analysis.

### 📈 Sales & Profit Dashboard
