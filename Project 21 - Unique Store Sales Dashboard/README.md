# 🛒 Unique Store Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
Created an interactive Unique Store Sales Dashboard in Power BI to analyze sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product trends, and regional sales insights through dynamic visualizations.

The dashboard helps users monitor business growth, identify top-performing products, compare sales across regions, and gain actionable insights for smarter business decisions.

---

## 🚀 Dashboard Highlights
- Total Sales & Profit KPIs  
- Product-wise Sales Performance Analysis  
- Customer Purchase Insights  
- Regional & State-wise Sales Tracking  
- Monthly & Yearly Sales Trend Analysis  
- Revenue & Profit Monitoring  
- Order & Quantity Analysis

## 🎯 Purpose
The objective of this project is to transform store sales data into meaningful business insights using interactive dashboards and analytical reporting techniques.

This dashboard helps businesses and analysts:
- Analyze overall sales performance  
- Monitor profit and revenue growth  
- Identify top-selling products  
- Track customer purchasing trends  
- Support strategic business decisions through data visualization
