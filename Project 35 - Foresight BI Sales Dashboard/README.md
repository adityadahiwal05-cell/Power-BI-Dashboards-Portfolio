# 📊 Foresight BI Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
Created an interactive Sales Analytics Dashboard in Power BI to analyze sales performance, revenue trends, customer insights, and business growth through dynamic visualizations.

The dashboard helps businesses monitor KPIs, identify profitable opportunities, and make data-driven decisions for improved sales performance and strategic planning.

---

## 🚀 Dashboard Highlights
- Total Sales, Profit & Revenue KPIs
- Monthly Sales Trend Analysis
- Customer Purchase Insights
- Product-wise Performance Tracking
- Region-wise Sales Analysis
- Profitability & Growth Monitoring
- Business Performance Visualization

## 🎯 Purpose
The objective of this project is to transform sales and business data into meaningful insights using interactive dashboards and analytical reporting techniques.

This dashboard helps analyze revenue trends, monitor business performance, track customer behavior, and support data-driven business decision-making.
