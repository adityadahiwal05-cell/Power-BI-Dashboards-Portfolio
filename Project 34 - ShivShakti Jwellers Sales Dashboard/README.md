# 💍 ShivShakti Jewellers Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
Created an interactive Jewellery Sales Analytics Dashboard in Power BI to analyze jewellery sales performance, gold & silver product trends, payment insights, Pune area-wise sales distribution, and overall business growth through dynamic visualizations.

The dashboard helps jewellery businesses monitor KPIs, understand customer purchasing behavior, track product demand, and make data-driven decisions for improved profitability and operational efficiency.

---

# 📑 Dashboard Pages Explanation

## 📊 Slide 1 – ShivShakti Jewellers Sales Dashboard
The first dashboard slide provides a complete overview of jewellery sales performance, monthly trends, metal-wise sales insights, and Pune area-wise business distribution.

### 🔹 Key Insights Covered
- Total Orders, Sales Amount & Quantity Sold
- Gold vs Silver Sales Comparison
- Monthly Sales Performance
- Pune Area-wise Sales Distribution
- Sales by Quantity Analysis
- Area-wise Revenue Insights

This slide helps businesses monitor overall jewellery sales performance and identify high-performing areas and products.
