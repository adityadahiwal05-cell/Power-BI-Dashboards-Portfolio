# 🛒 Universal Super Store Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
Created an interactive Super Store Analytics Dashboard in Power BI to analyze sales performance, profit trends, customer behavior, and business insights through dynamic visualizations.

The dashboard helps businesses monitor sales KPIs, identify profitable categories, and make data-driven decisions for improved business growth and operational performance.

---

## 🚀 Dashboard Highlights
- Total Sales, Profit & Quantity KPIs
- Category-wise Sales Analysis
- Region-wise Business Performance
- Monthly Sales & Profit Trends
- Customer Purchase Insights
- Shipping & Order Analysis
- Business Growth Visualization

## 🎯 Purpose
The objective of this project is to transform super store sales and customer data into meaningful business insights using interactive dashboards and analytical reporting techniques.

This dashboard helps analyze sales trends, monitor shipping performance, track customer behavior, and support data-driven business decision-making.
